NSE Momentum Screener

Get started with systematic momentum investing

This guide walks you through everything you need — from first-time setup to your first rebalance. Read it once, and the workflow becomes second nature.

What is this screener?

This screener scans the entire NSE-listed universe — over 2,000 stocks — and ranks them using a proprietary momentum score. It then applies a set of filters to eliminate stocks that are too risky: too illiquid, too volatile, losing trend strength, or under regulatory scrutiny. What remains is a clean, ranked shortlist of the strongest momentum stocks in the market right now.

Beyond surfacing stocks, it also tells you exactly what to do with them — which ones to hold, which to replace, and in what quantities — every single month.

💡
Rule-based investing vs. stock picking

Stock picking can work, but it's an art — shaped by experience, instinct, and sometimes luck. This screener turns that into a science: the same rules, applied consistently, every time, with no emotion. Consistency is what creates predictable returns over the long run.

Who is this for?

This is designed for investors who already understand the basics of markets — you've tried a few strategies on your own, you're comfortable holding equities, and you're ready to take a more structured, disciplined approach.

It is not a passive index fund. Momentum is a high-conviction, high-turnover strategy. When markets trend well, the returns can be significantly above the index. When markets chop or reverse sharply, drawdowns can be deeper. Make sure you're at peace with that tradeoff before investing.

⚠️
This is a high-risk, high-reward strategy

Best suited to investors with at least some prior market experience, a multi-year time horizon, and the discipline to stick to the system during drawdowns.

Before you begin

You need two things set up before the screener is of any use to you.

🏦 A broking account (e.g. Zerodha)
📱 Smallcase app, linked to your broker
💰 At least ₹1–2 lakh ready to invest

The Smallcase platform is where you'll actually hold and manage your portfolio. Think of the screener as your research engine — it tells you what to buy and sell and how many shares. Smallcase is where you store the portfolio, track performance, and execute the changes.

Once your broker account is active and linked to Smallcase, you're ready.

First-time setup

Do this once when you're starting from scratch. After this, the monthly rebalance workflow takes over.

1
Open the screener and read the Top 15

When the screener loads, you'll see a ranked list of stocks that have passed all filters. The top 15 are your starting portfolio. Note these down — these are the stocks you'll create your Smallcase with.

2
Create a Smallcase with those 15 stocks

Open the Smallcase app and tap Create Smallcase. Search for each of the 15 stocks and add them. Set all positions to equal weight — Smallcase will calculate the exact allocation automatically based on the amount you invest. Confirm and invest your amount. Smallcase places all orders through your linked broker at once.

3
Add GOLDBEES for the gold hedge (optional but recommended)

While creating the Smallcase, add GOLDBEES and set it to 10% of the total portfolio. This carves out a gold hedge, separate from the 15 equity positions. See the GOLDBEES section for the full rationale.

4
You're live

Once all orders fill, your momentum portfolio is active. Come back in roughly a month for your first rebalance. You can track performance any time in the Smallcase app.

Reading the screen

The screener runs automatically every trading day, around 4:30 PM IST — one hour after NSE closes. Each run fetches fresh price data, recomputes scores across the full universe, and updates the ranked list.

You don't need to analyse the numbers deeply — the filtering has already done the heavy work. The rank is the bottom line: the higher a stock sits, the stronger its momentum signal right now.

Monthly rebalance

Once invested, you rebalance roughly once a month. The goal is to exit stocks that have lost momentum and replace them with new ones that have risen in the rankings. Precision isn't the point — 28 days or 33 days is fine.

📅
Pick a day and be consistent

Consistency matters more than timing. Pick a rough day of the month that works for you and stick to it. If possible, avoid rebalancing on major event days like RBI policy announcements or the Union Budget.

The rebalance workflow, step by step

1
Export your portfolio from Smallcase

Open Smallcase on desktop (the export feature is desktop-only). Find your smallcase and export it as a CSV file. This tells the screener exactly what you currently hold and in what quantities.

2
Import the CSV into the screener

In the screener, switch to the Portfolio tab. Upload the CSV you just downloaded. The screener parses your current holdings and maps them against the latest run's rankings.

3
Review the Rebalance Delta (Phase 1)

Go to the Rebalance Delta tab. This is your review step. Every stock is tagged as Hold, Sell, or Buy. Look through the list — if you have a strong reason to override a call, you can flip the badge. Otherwise, trust the system. The screener's anti-whipsaw logic means it won't sell something that's merely dipped temporarily.

4
Generate the execution plan (Phase 2)

Once you're satisfied with Phase 1, confirm. The screener generates a precise execution plan: for every action, it shows you the exact number of shares to transact, calculated from your total current portfolio value. No mental arithmetic needed.

5
Execute in Smallcase

Take the execution plan to Smallcase and open Manage Smallcase.

Sell Find the stock in your smallcase. Set its quantity to 0. Smallcase will sell the entire position.
Buy Use the search bar to add the new stock. Enter the quantity from the execution plan.

Confirm all changes together. Smallcase places every order through your broker in one go.

✅
You're done

The whole process — from Smallcase export to execution — takes about 15–20 minutes once you've done it once or twice.

GOLDBEES & the gold sleeve

🥇
10% allocation to gold optional

GOLDBEES is a gold ETF listed on NSE — it tracks the price of physical gold and trades like any other stock. The screener sets aside a fixed 10% of your total portfolio for it, separate from the 15 equity positions. This is your hedge.

Gold and equities tend to move in opposite directions during periods of stress. When equity markets sell off sharply — which they will, at some point — gold typically rises. Holding 10% in gold means part of your portfolio is working in your favour during a downturn, reducing the severity of the overall drawdown.

This is optional. If you skip it, the equity portfolio simply runs at 100% of your investment. But the gold sleeve is recommended — it meaningfully smooths the ride without giving up much during bull markets.

How it rebalances

The screener tracks GOLDBEES separately. If gold drifts too far from the 10% target — because gold moved, or because your equity portfolio grew or shrank — the monthly rebalance plan will include a buy or sell instruction for GOLDBEES too, with an exact number of units. You handle it the same way as any other position in Smallcase.

Glossary

Plain-language definitions for terms used in the screener.

SMA21 / SMA200
Simple Moving Averages over 21 days and 200 days. The screener's core momentum score is the ratio of SMA21 ÷ SMA200. A high ratio means the recent trend is well above the long-term trend — strong upward momentum.
Momentum rank
A stock's position in the full NSE universe when sorted by momentum score, from highest to lowest. Rank #1 = the strongest momentum stock across all listed companies today.
Rank velocity
How many rank positions a stock has moved over the past ~13 days. A positive number (e.g. ▲ +9) means the stock has climbed 9 positions — its momentum is accelerating. Calculated from the statistical trend across the last 20 screen runs.
CMF
Chaikin Money Flow. Measures net buying vs. selling pressure over 20 days based on how prices close within each day's range. A CMF above 0.05 is required to pass the screener's filters — it indicates net institutional buying.
ADV
Average Daily Value traded (in rupees). A liquidity filter — the screener requires at least ₹10 crore of daily turnover. This ensures you can enter and exit positions at scale without significant slippage.
Hold zone
A buffer that protects existing positions from premature exits. A stock in your portfolio is only sold when it falls below rank 25 — not when it drops below rank 15. This prevents unnecessary churn from short-term rank fluctuations.
Near-high protection
If a held stock is within 5% of its 52-week high, the screener shields it from a sell call — shown with the 🛡 badge. Stocks near new highs frequently continue rising and selling them prematurely gives up upside.
Near-miss promotion
A stock that fails exactly one filter but only by a very small margin (≤10% of the threshold). The screener may include it in the top 15 if it ranks higher than a stock that passes all filters strictly. Shown with the ✨ badge.
Rebalance delta
The difference between what you currently hold and what the screener recommends. Expressed as Hold / Sell / Buy actions with exact share quantities for each stock.
GOLDBEES
A gold ETF listed on NSE that tracks the price of physical gold. Used as a 10% fixed allocation to hedge against equity market downturns — gold tends to rise when equity markets fall sharply.
Smallcase
A platform that lets you create and manage a basket of stocks as a single portfolio through your broker. Used here for portfolio creation, rebalancing, performance tracking, and investing more via lump sum or SIP.
SIP
Systematic Investment Plan. A fixed amount invested automatically at a set frequency (weekly, monthly, etc.). Available on the Smallcase platform for any smallcase you've created.
